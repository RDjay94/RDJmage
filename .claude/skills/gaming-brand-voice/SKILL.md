# Gaming Brand Voice & Tone

You define and protect a gaming brand's voice — the consistent personality across every word the brand publishes.

## When to use
- "Define our brand voice"
- "Is this copy on-brand?"
- "Adapt this for <channel>"
- "Write the voice & tone guide"

## Voice vs tone
- **Voice** is who the brand IS. It doesn't change.
- **Tone** is what the moment CALLS FOR. It flexes by channel + situation.

## Voice attributes framework
Pick 3–5 attributes. For each, define a "we are X / we are not Y" pair.

Example (default gaming-news brand, BigTaka-flavored):
- We are **knowledgeable**, not academic.
- We are **playful**, not silly.
- We are **fast**, not careless.
- We are **regional**, not parochial.
- We are **bilingual-native**, not auto-translated.

For each: 1 example sentence in voice + 1 sentence off-voice.

## Tone matrix
| Channel / situation | Tone shift |
|---|---|
| Social — meme / casual | Confident, witty, playful. Short. Emojis OK in moderation. |
| Social — patch / news | Direct, energetic. Facts first. |
| Support DMs / replies | Warm, calm, action-oriented. No corporate hedge. |
| Press release | Clear, factual, quotable. No marketing-speak. |
| Community announcements | Honest, transparent. We say what we know + don't. |
| In-game (UI, errors, FTUE) | Crisp, helpful. Each word earns its space. |
| Crisis comms | Plain, accountable, slow words, fast updates. (See `gaming-crisis-comms`.) |
| iGaming (compliant) | Restrained, entertainment-first, RG-aware. (See `casino-gaming-marketing`.) |

## Vocabulary do's and don'ts (default template — customize)
**Use**: ship, drop, season, queue, run it back, banger, "no notes", "we hear you", patch, build, lobby
**Avoid**: "exciting", "leverage", "synergy", "ecosystem", "world-class", "delight", "robust", "unleash"
**Banned**: anything that punches at players (skill issue, etc.), slurs (full list separate), unverified superlatives ("world's best")

## Bilingual style (BD default)
- English voice: confident, witty, fast
- Bengali voice: warm, vivid, culturally grounded (NEVER literal-translated English)
- Number formatting: Bengali numerals only in culturally-styled posts; Arabic numerals for stats, scores, prices
- Names: keep public figures' names in the script they themselves use
- Cricket / football / esports terms: use the form fans actually say (e.g. "ছক্কা" for six, never the academic equivalent)

## Length / pacing rules
- Tweet / X post: hook in line 1, payoff or twist in line 2, max 240 chars
- TikTok caption: 1–2 lines max
- Patch note headline: ≤8 words
- Support reply: ≤3 sentences before offering a next step
- Push notification: ≤10 words, action verb first

## Punctuation / formatting
- One space after a period.
- Exclamation marks: max one per post.
- Emojis: 0–2 per social post; never in press releases or support replies for serious issues.
- Caps for emphasis: rare; never SHOUTING.
- Em dash — used freely for energy.
- Oxford comma: yes.
- Bengali — follow Bangla Academy ০-৯ numerals where stylistic, otherwise standard Unicode rules.

## Audit a piece of copy (review checklist)
- [ ] Hook in the first line?
- [ ] Voice attributes present?
- [ ] Tone fits the channel?
- [ ] Banned phrases absent?
- [ ] Right length for surface?
- [ ] Localized (or flagged for translation)?
- [ ] CTA present (where applicable)?
- [ ] Accessibility: caption-burn for video, alt text for images?
- [ ] Compliance check on iGaming / regional rules?

## On / off-voice examples

Off-voice (too corporate):
> "We are excited to announce the launch of our latest update which brings a host of new exciting features to our incredible community."

On-voice:
> "Patch 1.4 is live. Three new modes, twelve fixes, one balance change you're going to fight about. Notes below ↓"

Off-voice (too edgelord):
> "Skill issue. Get good or get out."

On-voice (playful but warm):
> "Rough lobby? Same. Here's a thread of clutches from our worst weekend ever."

## Output format
For any voice request deliver:
1. Voice attributes (3–5) with "is / is not" pairs
2. Tone matrix per channel
3. Vocabulary do/don't list
4. Bilingual style notes (if applicable)
5. 2 on-voice + 2 off-voice examples
6. Audit checklist
7. Living-doc owner + review cadence (quarterly)

## Owner / governance
- One owner (Head of Brand or Marketing)
- Reviewed quarterly
- Linked from every other gaming-marketing skill — voice is upstream of all copy
