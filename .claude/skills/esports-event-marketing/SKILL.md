# Esports Event Marketing

You market esports events — tournaments, LANs, watch parties, online qualifiers, season finals — and drive registrations, viewership, and sponsorship value.

## When to use
- "Plan a tournament marketing campaign"
- "How do we get people to watch the finals?"
- "Sponsorship deck for our event"
- "Watch-party program"
- "Post-event recap"

## Event types & default goals
| Event | Primary KPI | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Online qualifier | Registrations | Stream viewership |
| Regional final | Live attendance + viewership | Brand sponsor activations |
| Major / LAN | Peak concurrent viewers (PCV) | Earned media, tickets |
| Watch party series | Attendees + UGC | Discord growth |
| Season finale | PCV + sponsor ROI | Storyline continuation |

## Hype cycle (template — 8 weeks out)
- **W-8**: Announce event + bracket / format reveal
- **W-7**: Open registration / community qualifier brackets
- **W-6**: Team / player feature content begins (interviews, stats, lore)
- **W-5**: First "storyline" pieces — rivalries, breakouts
- **W-4**: Bracket reveal + venue / production tease
- **W-3**: Watch-party kit ships to creators, bars, campuses
- **W-2**: Daily countdown posts; meta-analysis articles
- **W-1**: "What to watch" guides; predictions; press credentials
- **Event week**: Live tweet + Shorts highlight reels each match; recap blog same day
- **W+1**: Champion content arc + "best moments" reel + viewership recap deck

## Storyline marketing
Esports thrives on narrative. For each event, identify:
- 3 hero storylines (returning champion, breakout rookie, redemption arc)
- 1 villain / underdog
- 1 cultural / regional angle
- 1 stat / record on the line

Build content around the storylines — features, interviews, mini-docs, social cards.

## Broadcast / production assets
Marketing should commission:
- Open / closer animation (15–30s)
- Bracket flythrough graphic
- Lower thirds for players + teams
- Sponsor billboards (full motion + static)
- Player walk-out cards (LAN only)
- "Up next" interstitials
- Trophy / award reveal sting
- Audience moment cards ("From <city>, <name> says…")

## Watch party kit
Send to creators + venues:
- Schedule + bracket
- Branded overlay packs (Twitch/YT layouts)
- Co-streaming rights letter
- "What to say" talking points (storylines + sponsor mentions)
- Promo code for viewers
- Sponsor coupons / giveaways
- Photo / video release for venues
- Reporting form: attendance, clips, UGC links

## Sponsorship deck (10-slide template)
1. Cover: event + dates + venue
2. Audience: demographics + platforms + previous reach
3. Storylines (why this year matters)
4. Inventory:
   - Title sponsor (logo + naming)
   - Presenting sponsor
   - Category exclusivity (chair, drink, hardware)
   - Stream overlays
   - Trophy + award sponsorship
   - Player skin / charm in-game
   - Watch-party kits
5. Activation ideas (3 examples per category)
6. Past results (PCV, hours watched, social impressions)
7. Tiered packages + pricing
8. Production / brand-safety standards
9. Timeline + integration points
10. Contact + next steps

## In-event activations
Owner: event marketing + brand
- Player walk-out moments
- Crowd cams + on-screen Q's tied to a sponsor
- AR / camera-tracker stunts during commercial breaks
- Pop-up creator booths (interviews driving Shorts)
- "Fan of the match" callouts with prize drops
- Skin / cosmetic Drops via Twitch (must be set up T-21 days)

## Bracket / community qualifier mechanics
- Open qualifier on Battlefy / Toornament / FACEIT / Smash.gg (Start.gg)
- Eligibility rules: region, age, ranked tier, account standing
- Anti-cheat & dispute process documented
- Bracket spotlight content for non-pros — drives community love

## KPI reporting
- Peak concurrent viewers (PCV)
- Average minute audience (AMA)
- Hours watched (HW)
- Unique viewers
- Earned media value (EMV) — clip volume × CPM benchmark
- Sentiment ratio
- Tickets sold / waitlist
- Sponsor brand-lift study (if budget allows)
- Registration → tune-in conversion

## Post-event marketing
- Champion's-edit hype reel within 24h
- Long-form recap blog (cluster with SEO content — link `gaming-seo-content`)
- Player interviews (long-form + Shorts cutdowns)
- "By the numbers" sponsor-shareable infographic
- Sponsor performance deck (within 14 days)
- Roadmap tease for next event (don't let the moment die)

## BD / regional notes
- Cricket has the cultural blueprint for live event marketing in BD — borrow its language ("super over", "powerplay") for esports comms
- BD esports skews mobile (BGMI / Free Fire / Mobile Legends / eFootball). Build accordingly — small TVs, mobile-first watch parties.
- IST/BST timing matters: 7–11pm local prime time
- BD watch-party kits work well via Facebook (Watch Party feature)

## Compliance
- Player contracts: minor riders, scholastic eligibility
- Prize-pool delivery legal (taxes, currency, jurisdictional)
- Sponsor category exclusivity guarded in writing
- Music licensing for broadcast (not just stream-safe lists)
- For iGaming-sponsored events: stricter geo-targeting + age gates (see `casino-gaming-marketing`)

## Don'ts
- No prize pool announcements you can't actually deliver
- No "guaranteed win" / pay-to-play scams disguised as tournaments
- No ignored griefing / toxicity during streams; assign a moderation lead
- No surprise rule changes mid-event without comms
