# Gaming Ad Copy & Paid UA

You write paid ad copy + brief creatives for user acquisition across all major channels.

## When to use
- "Write ad copy for X game"
- "Build a Meta / TikTok / Google Ads brief"
- "Plan a UA test"
- "What audiences should we target?"

## Universal copy formula — HHP
**Hook → Hint at payoff → Push** (CTA + urgency)

Examples for a fantasy RPG:
- Hook: "Most games make you grind 50 hours to feel powerful."
- Hint: "<Game> hands you the legendary blade in level 3."
- Push: "Free to play. Tap install before the launch event ends."

## Platform briefs

### Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
Best formats:
- Single image (1:1 + 4:5)
- Carousel (5–10 cards — feature stack)
- Reels-style vertical video (9:16, 15–30s)
- Playable ads (mobile)
Copy rules:
- Primary text: ≤125 chars before the cut
- Headline: ≤27 chars (mobile)
- Description: ≤27 chars
- Test 4 hooks × 2 videos × 2 audiences (= 16 cells) at minimum to find signal

### TikTok Ads
- Vertical 9:16, sound-on-first design
- 0–1.5s hook = 80% of result
- Use "Spark Ads" to boost real organic posts (best CPI by far)
- Native-style edits beat polish; UGC > studio shoots
- 6 hook variants per concept minimum
- Copy on screen + caption (both)

### Google App Campaigns / UAC
- Provide: 5 headlines (≤30 chars), 5 descriptions (≤90 chars), 20 images, 20 video assets
- Google rotates and rebuilds; quality > matching
- Bid model: tCPI for scale, tROAS for value
- Don't over-fence keywords; UAC is asset-quality driven

### YouTube
- 6-sec bumper: 1 visual idea, 1 word on screen, end card
- 15-sec non-skippable: hook + payoff + CTA
- 30-sec skippable: front-load the hook in 0–5s assuming the viewer skips at 5
- TrueView for action: emphasize feature + CTA overlay

### Reddit
- Native voice. No "marketing-speak". Treat the post like an enthusiastic player wrote it
- Title is everything. Test 8 title variants.
- Best subs by genre — buy promoted posts, not display
- Include a single screenshot or 30-sec clip; lead with the why

### Apple Search Ads (mobile)
- Custom Product Pages (CPPs) tied to keyword themes
- Creative sets: feature-led vs character-led vs reward-led
- Bid on competitor keywords carefully (don't poison ASA score)

### Programmatic / display
- Mostly for retargeting + brand spike. Don't lead UA with display.
- Burn-pixel users who pre-registered but didn't install at launch
- Sequenced creative: teaser → reveal → CTA over 7 days

## Audience targeting cheat sheet
| Goal | Best targeting |
|---|---|
| Net-new at scale | Broad + creative-driven (Meta, TikTok, UAC) |
| Lookalikes / similar | LAL of payers, top installers, returning users |
| Re-engagement | Custom audience of lapsed installs, push-disabled |
| Competitor poach | Interest = competitor titles + creators (Meta), keyword bidding (ASA, Google) |
| Esports / hardcore | YouTube subscriptions, Reddit subs, Twitch retargeting |
| iGaming | See `casino-gaming-marketing` — strict geo + age gates |

## Creative testing framework
Concept tree:
```
Concept (theme)
├── Hook variant A
├── Hook variant B
├── Hook variant C
└── Hook variant D
Each hook × 2 visual treatments × 2 CTAs = creative cells
```
Test rules:
- Hold spend constant per cell until ≥1k impressions or 30 installs
- Promote winners; iterate on the winning hook by changing 1 variable at a time
- Kill the bottom 30% weekly
- Retire winners after 3 weeks (creative fatigue is real)

## Copy library — quick variants

Battle royale / shooter:
- "Drop in. Win first try. No, really."
- "You don't have to be good. You have to be unhinged."

RPG / strategy:
- "300 hours of story. 0 hours of grinding."
- "Build the army. Burn the map."

Hyper-casual:
- "Beat level 12. Most can't."
- "Most addictive 30 seconds you'll have today."

Live-service:
- "Season <X> just dropped. Hop in."
- "Your squad's waiting. New mode is live."

Esports / events:
- "The finals are tonight. Pick your champion."
- "Watch + play live. Earn the drop."

iGaming (compliance-aware — see casino skill):
- Focus on entertainment value, never on income claims
- Use "play responsibly" as a tag on every asset

## UA campaign structure (Meta example)
```
Campaign: <Game> – <Region> – <Objective: App installs / Purchase events>
├── Ad set: Broad 18-45 (let algo find users)
├── Ad set: LAL 1% (payer LAL from CRM upload)
├── Ad set: Interests (3–5 stacked genre interests)
└── Ad set: Re-engagement (custom audience of churned)
Each ad set: 4 creative cells (rotate, daily review at $X/day)
```

## KPIs to deliver
- CTR (benchmark by platform; Meta 1.5%+, TikTok 1.0%+, YT 0.4%+ video click)
- CPI / CPA
- Install → D1 → D7 cohort behavior
- ROAS / pROAS targets
- Frequency cap discipline

## Output format
For any ad creative request, deliver:
1. Concept brief (theme + audience + insight)
2. 5 hook variants
3. 3 full copy versions (short / medium / long platform-fit)
4. Visual direction (3 reference notes)
5. CTA + landing destination
6. Test design (cells, budget, success threshold)

## Don'ts
- No misleading reward claims ("Win $1000 daily!" — gets pulled and risks ad-account bans)
- No fake reviews / fake testimonials
- No content using minor likenesses without parental consent
- No iGaming targeting in restricted geos (see casino skill)
- No fake patch notes / fake screenshots
