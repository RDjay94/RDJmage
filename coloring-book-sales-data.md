## Per-Book Royalty Economics

| Price Point | Royalty per Sale | Monthly Sales Needed for $1,000/mo |
|:-----------:|:----------------:|:----------------------------------:|
| $6.99 | ~$1.50 | 667 copies |
| $7.99 | ~$1.95 | 513 copies |
| $9.99 | ~$2.90 | 345 copies |
| $12.99 | ~$4.10 | 244 copies |

### After June 2025 KDP Changes

| Price Point | Royalty per Sale | Monthly Sales Needed for $1,000/mo |
|:-----------:|:----------------:|:----------------------------------:|
| $6.99 | ~$0.85 | 1,176 copies |
| $7.99 | ~$1.16 | 862 copies |
| $9.99 | ~$2.50 | 400 copies |
| $12.99 | ~$3.80 | 264 copies |

> **Key takeaway:** The $9.99 price point is now the sweet spot for profitability after the June 2025 royalty changes.

## Sales Seasonality

| Period | Sales Impact | Best-Selling Categories |
|--------|:------------:|------------------------|
| **Nov-Dec** (Holiday) | **+150-200%** peak | Gift sets, kids' coloring, themed holiday books |
| **Jan-Feb** | +30% (New Year wellness) | Stress relief, mindfulness, adult mandala |
| **Mar-Apr** (Spring) | Baseline | Nature, flowers, garden themes |
| **May** (Mother's Day) | +40% spike | Floral, inspirational quotes, gift-worthy designs |

## Year-over-Year Growth Highlights

- **2024 → 2025:** Adult coloring book market grew ~6.5% globally
- **Brazil** saw the largest single-country surge — coloring books dominated the overall Amazon.com.br bestseller list in early 2025
- **Bold & Easy** style went from niche to 40% of US bestsellers in under 18 months
- **Online sales** crossed 52% of total distribution for the first time
- **Asia Pacific** maintained 7.2% CAGR, with India leading growth at 9.2%
